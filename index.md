# YummyBait

YummyBait is a DeFi analytics platform for Uniswap V3 and V4 — real-time
indexing, position tracking, risk scoring, a Telegram bot for
LPs, and (coming soon) signals you can poll into your own agents.

This site documents the **public surface**:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-robot:{ .lg .middle } **Telegram Bot**

    ---

    Add your wallet, watch your positions, get AI-powered advice on when
    to act.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Get started](bot/index.md)

- :material-webhook:{ .lg .middle } **Signals**

    ---

    Poll your own agent against the metrics the LLM advisor sees, using
    rules you define. Pull-based — no callback URL to run.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Get started](signals/index.md)
